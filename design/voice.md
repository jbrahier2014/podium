# Voice

Podium sounds like the friend on the next seat who actually knows the table: quick, specific, a little competitive, never shouting.

## Principles

- **Lead with the result.** "North Harbor win 2–1" before any context. Scores, minutes and positions come first.
- **Specific over hype.** "Third straight clean sheet" beats "Incredible defending!". No exclamation marks in UI, no ALL-CAPS sentences, no emoji in product copy.
- **Short.** Headlines under 60 characters, notifications under 90, button labels 1–3 words.
- **Even-handed.** Report both sides the same way. Celebrate the viewer's team only in their own space (My teams, their notifications).

## Mechanics

- Sentence case everywhere, including buttons and headings. Overlines and badges are uppercased by CSS — type them in sentence case.
- Scores: home first, en dash with no spaces in text ("2–1"); in components the score sits beside each team.
- Clock: minutes with a prime ("67'"), added time as "90+3'". Results: FT, AET, Pens.
- Times in the viewer's local 24-hour or 12-hour setting; dates as "Sat 14 Jun".
- Numbers as numerals, always.

## Examples

| Do | Don't |
| --- | --- |
| Live · 67' | LIVE NOW!!! |
| Lakeport go top on goal difference | Massive drama at the top! |
| Kick-off moved to 20:00 | Heads up! Time change |
| Follow | Click to follow this team |
| Get notified | Never miss a moment! |
